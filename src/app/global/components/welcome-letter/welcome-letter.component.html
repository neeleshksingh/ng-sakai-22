Sarala Birla University is a self-financed private unitary university. The university was established by the great industrialist and philanthropist Shri B. K. Birla and his wife Smt. Sarala Birla whose vision was to build an Intelligent Citizen with a Commitment to Serve the Nation and Protect Mother Earth. The university runs with the word of wisdom of Smt. Sarala Birla which is “Education is meaningless ritual unless it moulds the character of student and give them values of life”, and hence discipline within the campus and a harmonious character is encouraged. The Management of the university is carried out by a Board of Governors headed by Smt. Jayashree Mohta, Honourable Chairperson, Bharat Arogya & Gyan Mandir and the Chancellor of Sarala Birla University.

The SBU, Ranchi campus is situated amidst the verdant surroundings, stretching over nearly 50 acres of land owned by Bharat Arogya & Gyan Mandir at Birla Campus (Village Ara, P.O. Mahilong, Ranchi-Purulia Highway).

SBU imparts quality education in various fields viz.
