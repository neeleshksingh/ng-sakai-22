Strict Prohibition of Four-Wheeler Parking on Public Roads and Current On-Campus Restrictions

This circular is issued to inform all students regarding the current policy concerning the parking of four-wheeled vehicles.

1. On-Campus Parking Status

Please note that the institution's premises DO NOT currently have designated parking facilities available for students' four-wheelers.

2. Strict Prohibition on Public Road Parking (Mandatory Adherence)

Students are STRICTLY PROHIBITED from parking their four-wheelers on the public roads, lanes, or streets adjacent to or surrounding the campus premises. This measure is essential to ensure public safety, maintain smooth traffic flow, and comply with local municipal regulations.

Any vehicle found parked in violation of this directive on public access roads may be subject to towing or disciplinary action. The institution will not be responsible for any resultant penalties, damages, or losses incurred by the student owner.

3. Future Facilitation

The administration is actively assessing possibilities to facilitate limited on-campus parking for four-wheelers in the future. Students will be notified immediately through official channels once a viable and structured solution is implemented and becomes available.

Until such a time, all students who use four-wheelers are required to make alternative arrangements for parking their vehicles off-site, away from the immediate campus vicinity and surrounding public access roads.

Your cooperation in ensuring a safe and organized environment is highly appreciated.
