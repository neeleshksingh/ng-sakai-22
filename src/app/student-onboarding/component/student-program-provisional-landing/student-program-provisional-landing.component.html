Fee Refund Policy - Academic Year 2025-26
(In reference to UGC Circular No. F.2-71/2022 (CPP-II) (C-114546), dated 12th June 2024)
1. Notification to Student

In reference to the University Grants Commission (UGC) Circular No. F.2-71/2022 (CPP-II) (C-114546) dated 12th June 2024, issued for the academic year 2024-25, all Student are hereby informed that the new UGC Refund Circular for 2025-26 is awaited.

Accordingly, the refund policy from the previous academic year shall continue to remain in force until further notification from UGC. Please note that the last dates mentioned may be subject to change based on forthcoming UGC updates.

2. Refund Provisions (As per UGC Guidelines)

Students cancelling their admission will be entitled to refunds as per the timeline and deductions below:

Sl. No Date of Admission Withdrawal Refund Amount
1 15 days or more before the last date of admission 100% of fees paid
2 Less than 15 days before the last date of admission 90% of fees paid
3 Within 15 days after the last date of admission 80% of fees paid
4 Between 16 to 30 days after the last date of admission 50% of fees paid
5 After 30 days from the last date of admission No refund applicable
Note:

1. For all cancellations up to 30th September 2025, a full refund shall be provided.

2. Cancellations between 1st October and 31st October 2025 will be eligible for a refund with ₹1,000 deduction.

3. No refund shall be applicable from 1st November 2025 onwards.

3. Mode of Refund

All refunds shall be processed through bank transfer to the account of the student or parent/guardian, as per the admission records.

4. Ancillary Charges (Hostel, Transport, etc.)

Fees related to hostel, mess, and transportation services will be refunded on a pro-rata basis, based on the duration of availed services, and as per the specific department's policy.

5. Review Clause

This policy will remain in effect until the release of the updated UGC Refund Guidelines for 2025-26. Any changes to the refund structure or last dates will be communicated as and when the new UGC circular is issued.
